🎉 Events

Manual events shown on the staff portal — private parties, buyouts, happenings that don't sell tickets. "Block during event" closes Wix online reservations from 1 hr before the start time until the end time at that location; "Block all day" closes the whole date. Ticketed classes & events live under Tickets & Classes.

🗓️ Reservations

Live from Wix. Add a phone-in or walk-in booking (created confirmed — the guest gets Wix's confirmation if you enter contact info), or click a reservation to change its time, party size, guest info, or cancel it. Requests still get approved or declined on the staff page.

🚫 Blocked Dates — Online Reservations

During these windows the Wix reservation widget shows no availability at the selected location (set automatically by portal events, or add one manually for a buyout or private party). Phone-in bookings added here still work. If the end time is earlier than the start time, the block runs into the next day.

🔁 Swaps waiting on you

A coworker accepted these shift offers — they only take effect once you approve. Approving moves the shift and notifies both employees.

🗓️ Weekly Schedule — all locations

All three schedules for the week below. Nashville and Franklin share one staff pool — both grids show the combined roster, and a shift belongs to the grid you add it on. Click any cell to add a shift; drag a shift to another day or employee to copy it there (the original stays put); click one to edit, delete or copy it. Approved time off shows as a red OFF badge, and staff availability shows as grey unavail / green ★ chips. Edits stay private until you hit 📣 Publish & notify — that emails everyone whose week changed. Staff open their schedule at schedule.html with their employee ID.

🧑‍🍳 Employees

Employee ID is what staff type to open their schedule (no password) — it can be anything: a number, initials, a nickname. Set someone inactive to hide them from the grid without deleting their history. Click a row to edit.
