🎉 Events

Manual events shown on the staff portal — private parties, buyouts, happenings that don't sell tickets. "Block during event" closes Wix online reservations from 1 hr before the start time until the end time at that location; "Block all day" closes the whole date. Ticketed classes & events live under Tickets & Classes.

💬 Experience Inquiries

Questions customers send from the Experiences page (Ask a question). Reply here and it's emailed to them from info@greyscheese.com; their replies go to brad@greyscheese.com and are copied in here automatically every 5 minutes (Gmail sync script). Log their reply is there for anything that comes in another way (text, phone). Booking link drops in a link that opens their experience already filled in.

💼 Catering Leads

Requests from tickets.greyscheese.com/catering.html (and any you add by hand). Reply here and it's emailed from info@greyscheese.com; their replies come back in automatically (Gmail sync). Build the Proposal, send it, and the customer signs online — that creates a Square invoice (deposit now, balance before the event) with the signed agreement attached. Payment status refreshes from Square every 15 minutes.

🔁 Swaps waiting on you

A coworker accepted these shift offers — they only take effect once you approve. Approving moves the shift and notifies both employees.

🗓️ Weekly Schedule — all locations

Both schedules for the week below. A shift belongs to the grid you add it on. Click any cell to add a shift; drag a shift to another day or employee to copy it there (the original stays put); click one to edit, delete or copy it. Approved time off shows as a red OFF badge, and staff availability shows as grey unavail / green ★ chips. Edits stay private until you hit 📣 Publish & notify — that emails everyone whose week changed. Staff open their schedule at schedule.html with their employee ID.

🧑‍🍳 Employees

Employee ID is what staff type to open their schedule (no password) — it can be anything: a number, initials, a nickname. Set someone inactive to hide them from the grid without deleting their history. Click a row to edit.
